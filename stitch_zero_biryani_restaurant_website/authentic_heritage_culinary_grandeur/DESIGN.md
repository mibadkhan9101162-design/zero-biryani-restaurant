## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-end hospitality identity that pairs a decade-long heritage of slow-cooked dum biryani with the refined editorial presence of luxury dining. The visual tone evokes regal warmth, aromatic richness, culinary authenticity, and dignified celebration. 

The aesthetic is an editorial synthesis of **Modern Editorial Luxury** and **Warm Tactility**. It pairs generous margins, bespoke typographic scale, and delicate hairline gold borders with evocative high-contrast surface transitions: shifting seamlessly between deep, slow-simmer charcoal chambers (for celebratory, dramatic moments like the hero, private dining, and footer) and buttery, sunlit ivory plains (for menus, stories, and table reservations). The interface eschews cold digital sterility in favor of spice-toned accents, warm ambient glows, and precise culinary craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette mirrors the alchemical transformation of fragrant basmati, hand-ground saffron, and slow-roasted spices cooked under sealed clay purdahs.

- **Primary (Warm Golden Saffron - `#D4A359`, `#E5B869`, `#C59141`):** Represents rich saffron threads, aged brass ware, and culinary mastery. Used for prominent badges, bespoke decorative dividers, active navigation markers, star ratings, and key monetary amounts.
- **Secondary (Spiced Pomegranate / Deep Burgundy - `#872023`, `#72181B`):** Conveys depth, slow simmer, and intense flavor. Reserved strictly for intentional high-conversion touchpoints, signature chef specials, spice-level indicators, and urgent interaction badges.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces (Dual-World Canvas):**
  - *Day/Menu Realm:* `#FDFBF7` (Warm Ivory) as root canvas, layered with `#F7F3EA` (Rich Cream) for cards and grouped content. Text relies on `#1E1C1A` (Deep Charcoal) for primary readability and `#5C564E` (Warm Roasted Earth) for secondary descriptors and metadata.
  - *Dusk/Heritage Realm:* `#121110` (Kohl Charcoal) as the stage for heroes, culinary vignettes, storytelling strips, and footers, accompanied by `#181614` for elevated surface containers and `#F5F5F5` (Crisp Off-White) for immaculate typographical contrast.
- **Borders & Filigree:** Delicate metallic traces using `rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.28)` ensure structural separation remains graceful without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a deliberate rhythm between classical literary luxury and frictionless contemporary utility.

- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display introduces grand culinary tradition, editorial dignity, and graceful drama. Large numerals (such as "10+ Years", item prices, and chronological years) leverage Playfair Display’s high stroke contrast.
- **Body & Controls:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides exceptional geometric legibility, balancing the expressive display type with understated clarity across menu descriptions, reservation flows, allergen callouts, and transactional steps.
- **Label Caps & Monograms:** Small uppercase labels paired with wide letter-spacing (`0.18em`) are used for prestigious categorization (e.g., "HAND-CRAFTED DUM RECIPES", "HERITAGE BATCH", "SEEDED 2014").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by a maximum width container of `1280px`. Generous vertical breathing room replicates the spacious cadence of high-end culinary menus.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, 64px (`4rem`) outer margins. Editorial multi-column menus, split tasting card showcases, and asymmetrical image locks.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer margins. Two-column menu presentations and condensed reservation modules.
  - **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margins. Single column vertical flow with horizontal snap-scroll carousels for chef recommendations.
- **Sectional Pacing:** Main thematic content sections alternate padding between `space-3xl` and `space-4xl` to allow food photography, spice stories, and dining rooms adequate grandeur.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects heavy, synthetic drop shadows in favor of **warm tonal layering**, **saffron ambient glows**, and **tactile gilded borders**.

- **Light Canvas Depth:** Raised cards on cream surfaces utilize a composite ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 28, 26, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 28, 26, 0.03)`, bounded by a 1px hairline border of `rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.22)`.
- **Dark Canvas Depth:** Elevated surfaces within dark sections utilize stepped tonal containers (`#181614` elevated to `#22201D`), accented with soft golden radial gradients (`radial-gradient(ellipse at top, rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.08), transparent 70%)`) and perimeter hairlines of `rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.35)`.
- **Modal & Floating Dialogs:** Float over a tinted backdrop overlay (`rgba(18, 17, 16, 0.85)` with `4px` blur) using a deep shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (`1`)** structural discipline. Sharp, clean geometry evokes fine architectural dining rooms and vintage parchment menus, subtly relaxed with precise corner curves:

- **Base Radius (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Maintains an authoritative, timeless structure.
- **Card & Container Radius:** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Keeps imagery framed with tailored poise.
- **Hero & Feature Media:** Framed either with soft `8px` corners or enveloped in classic arched profiles (`border-radius: 240px 240px 8px 8px`) reflecting Mughal architectural arches and royal banquet alcoves.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted strictly to micro-status indicators (e.g., dietary icons, live spice scales) to contrast against rectangular menu blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Culinary Gold):** Solid saffron `#D4A359` background with `#121110` text, `4px` border radius, uppercase tracking (`0.14em`), font weight 600. Hover transitions to `#E5B869` with a subtle elevation lift.
- **Specialty CTA (Spiced Pomegranate):** Rich `#872023` background with `#F5F5F5` text, used for high-intent actions ("Reserve A Banquet Table", "Order Handi Now"). Hover darkens to `#72181B`.
- **Ghost / Editorial Action:** Transparent base, `1px` border in `#C59141` or `#1E1C1A`, matching typography, with interior fill gently appearing on hover.

### Menu Cards & Recipe Items
- **Card Anatomy:** Split layout with dish photography, title in Playfair Display, price in saffron numeral style, and ingredient descriptions in roasted earth tone (`#5C564E`).
- **Purdah Dum Indicators:** Specialized gold-leaf badges (`rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.15)` background, `#C59141` text) indicating pot-sealed duration, portion sizes, or decade-old signature status.

### Chips & Badges
- **Heritage Pill:** Hairline gold stroke, warm ivory or dark charcoal fill, featuring `label-caps` typography ("10 YR TRADITION", "MATKA DUM", "CHEF'S CUT").
- **Dietary Badges:** Minimal geometric squares with subtle color-coding (Forest Umber for vegetarian items, Spiced Pomegranate for slow-cooked meats).

### Form Inputs & Reservation Pickers
- **Text & Date Fields:** Set on `#F7F3EA` (light mode) or `#181614` (dark mode), bounded by a subtle `1px` border of `rgba(92, 86, 78, 0.3)`. When focused, transitions to a defined `1px` saffron border (`#D4A359`) with zero harsh outer halo.
- **Labels:** Set inside or floating above inputs using `label-md` in `#5C564E` or `#D4A359`.

### Food Detail Modal & Drawer
- An architectural presentation featuring an arched top image gallery, authentic spice breakdown, allergen notes, and fixed-bottom reservation or ordering bar framed in deep charcoal `#181614` with saffron highlights.
